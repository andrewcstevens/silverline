How it works. The Wizard pulls Coinbase BTC-USD 15-minute candles (aligned to :00/:15/:30/:45 of each hour), keeps weekdays, and computes the close-to-close return for each 15-minute snapshot. Slots are split into a Day session (8a–8p ET) and a Night session (8p–8a ET); each axis shows ET on top with your San Francisco (Pacific) time beneath — PT = ET − 3h. "Win %" is the historical share of snapshots that moved in the favored direction; 95% confidence intervals use the Wilson score method. The Buy Signal shows what you'd win and your expected value, and picks the next upcoming slot with a positive edge from anywhere in the 24h day.

Honest caveat. This is a historical backtest and probability tool, not financial advice. Bitcoin is a near-random walk at the 15-minute scale — overall the market has been roughly 50.4% up on weekdays over the sample, and although the strongest edges cluster in the overnight/low-liquidity hours, most time-of-day edges are not statistically significant once you account for sample size and multiple comparisons. Treat any single slot's win rate as a weak, regime-dependent signal, never a guarantee. Past performance does not predict future results. Data source: Coinbase Exchange public API (BTC-USD).
